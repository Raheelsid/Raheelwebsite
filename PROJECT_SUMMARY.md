## 📧 Contact Form Setup

### Recommended: Formspree (Simplest)
1. Go to https://formspree.io
2. Sign up (free)
3. Create new form
4. Get your form ID
5. Update form action in index.html (line ~241):
```html
<form class="contact-form" action="https://formspree.io/f/YOUR_ID" method="POST">
```

That's it! Your form will work immediately.
